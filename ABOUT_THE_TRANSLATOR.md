# 关于译者 / About the Translator

郁江（Ikue）是《爱与幻想的法西斯主义》首部完整中文译本计划的发起人、主导译者与总审定者，负责制定翻译方向，处理关键译名、语义和文体选择，审读并修改中文译文，以及决定文本是否进入稳定版本。

本仓库记录郁江与该翻译项目的关系、公开时间及阶段性版本。全书尚处于核校、审读和修订阶段。

在全书核校、必要的实质性修改和最终审定完成后，稳定版拟采用以下署名：

> 村上龙 著<br>
> 郁江 译

具体工作分工另见 [AI_DISCLOSURE.md](AI_DISCLOSURE.md)。

## English Bio

Ikue (郁江) initiated and leads a project intended to produce the first publicly released complete Chinese translation of Ryū Murakami's *The Fascism of Love and Fantasy*. Ikue establishes the translation approach, resolves key decisions concerning names, meaning and literary style, reviews and revises the Chinese text, and determines whether it is ready for a stable edition.

The manuscript is under review and revision. This repository records project leadership and version history; it does not itself establish historical priority or announce a published edition.
